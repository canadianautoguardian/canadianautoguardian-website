Common questions

Good To Know

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

Domestic, import (including Japanese and European), hybrid, electric and exotic vehicles. Rates depend on the vehicle type, and you'll get an estimate before any work begins.

Do you service hybrids and electric vehicles?

Yes. We service hybrid and electric vehicles at $150/hr.

How much do you charge?

Our hourly rates start at $130/hr depending on your vehicle type, and you'll get an estimate before any work begins. See all rates by vehicle type.

My insurer recommended a different shop. Can I still come to you?

Yes. Choosing your repair shop is your right. Your insurer may suggest a shop, but you don't have to use it. Just tell your adjuster it's us.

Can you help arrange a tow after a collision?

Yes. We handle the tow, the adjuster and the repair. Our collision claim guide walks you through every step.

My check engine light is on. Can you find out why?

Yes. We diagnose warning lights, noises and performance problems to find the cause before any repair begins.
