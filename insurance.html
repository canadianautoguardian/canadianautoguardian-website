Customer claim guide

Had A Collision?
Here's What To Do.

Twelve steps to a smooth Ontario insurance claim. We work with all insurance providers. Print it and keep it in your glovebox.

Call 1-877-766-3457
Anyone hurt? Call 911 Over $2,000 damage? Report within 24 hrs Then call us: 1-877-766-3457
Phase 01

At The Scene

  1. 1

    Make The Scene Safe

    Hazards on. Call 911 if anyone's hurt or impaired, or a car can't move.

  2. 2

    Exchange Information

    Name, phone, licence, plate, insurer and policy number. Witness contacts too.

  3. 3

    Document Everything

    Photos of every vehicle, plate and the scene. Don't admit fault or sign anything.

  4. 4

    Report If Required

    Injuries or $2,000+ combined damage: report at a Collision Reporting Centre.

  5. 5

    Choose Your Tow Your Right

    Ask for the certificate and rates first. Send the car straight to us.

Phase 02

Start Your Claim

  1. 6

    Call Your Insurer

    As soon as possible, generally within 7 days. Save your claim number.

  2. 7

    Pick Your Shop Your Right

    You don't have to use the insurer's shop. Tell the adjuster it's us.

  3. 8

    Damage Assessment

    We send the adjuster our estimate and photos, and handle hidden damage.

  4. 9

    Ask About A Rental

    Coverage depends on your policy. Confirm your daily limit before renting.

Phase 03

Back On The Road

  1. 10

    Fault And Deductible

    Set under Ontario's rules. Not at fault usually means no deductible.

  2. 11

    Repair And Pick-Up

    Inspect the car with us, pay any deductible, keep your warranty papers.

  3. 12

    Disagree? Escalate

    Ask for reasons in writing, then the insurer's ombudsman, then GIO or FSRA.
