About Canadian Auto Guardian

Your auto repair shop on Heritage Road in Markham.

Our story

Who We Are

Canadian Auto Guardian is an auto repair shop on Heritage Road in Markham, led by president Dishan Ramnanan, who brings years of hands-on experience working on vehicles. We keep Markham drivers on the road, from routine oil changes, brakes and tune-ups to engine repairs, diagnostics and A/C work.

We service domestic, import, hybrid, electric, and exotic and specialty vehicles. Whatever you drive, you get an estimate before any repair begins, so you know what to expect.

When the unexpected happens, we're on your side. We work with all insurance providers and handle the tow, the adjuster and the repair, so you can get back on the road with confidence.

What to expect

Working With Us

  • All insurance providers welcome. Whoever insures your vehicle, we can work with them.
  • Easy to reach. Call, email, or request an appointment online.
  • Convenient hours. Open weekdays 9–5 and Saturdays 9–2.
  • Estimates before repairs. You'll know what the work involves and the estimated cost before we start.
